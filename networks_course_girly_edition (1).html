What is Network? (ما هي الشبكة ولماذا نحتاجها؟)

💻

What is Network? Page 5

Network: is a group of connected computers and devices.
الشبكة هي مجموعة من أجهزة الكمبيوتر والأجهزة الإلكترونية المتصلة ببعضها.

Example:

The computers, phones, and other devices that your family owns are all part of your home computer network.

When you get a new tablet and enter in the Wi-Fi password, you're joining that tablet to your home computer network.

🔗

Why do we need Network? Page 5

Devices in the same network can easily communicate and share resources with each other.
الأجهزة في نفس الشبكة تستطيع التواصل بسهولة ومشاركة الموارد مع بعضها.

For example:

  • You can control your TV with your mobile.
  • You can stream videos from your mobile to your TV.

Network Types (أنواع الشبكات)

Type 1
🏠

1- LAN

Local Area Network

LAN Definition (PDF Page 9):

Local Area Network that has a group of connected devices. LAN is a type of network made up of nearby computers.

Key Features:

  • A LAN can have anywhere from a few devices (like a home network) to hundreds of devices (like a school or hospital).
  • Large organizations like colleges can have multiple LANs - maybe one per building.
Type 2
🌍

2- WAN

Wide Area Network

WAN Definition (PDF Page 14):

WAN is made of connected LANs that are far from each other (like in different parts of the country or world).

Key Features:

  • WANs connect thousands or even millions of devices.
  • Connects multiple separate LANs over long distances (PDF Page 15 diagram).

Communicating on LAN (طرق التواصل في الشبكة المحلية)

🔌

1- Wired Connection

Using wires or cables with Ethernet

Ethernet Definition:

Ethernet: Rules that computers follow for communicating on a wired network.

Devices in Wired Networks (Page 12):

Switch Connects devices inside LAN
Router Connects networks to Internet
📶

2- Wireless Connection

Without wires using Wi-Fi

Wireless Rules:

Rules that computers follow for communicating through radio signals.

Wi-Fi:

Wi-Fi is a popular way to connect wirelessly to a network.

Wi-Fi stands for: Wireless Fidelity

What is Protocol? (PDF Page 12)

Protocol: is a standard set of rules for how devices communicate through wires or wireless.
